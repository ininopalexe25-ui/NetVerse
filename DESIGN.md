# Design System: NetVerse NOC & Cyber-Hardware Workbench

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Design Read
Reading this as: Interactive 3D Educational Laboratory for Vocational Teacher Students (PTI/TKJ), with an authentic Tactical Network Operations & Hardware Workbench language, leaning toward high-precision telecommunication rack interfaces, tactile port pinouts, and immersive WebGL 3D prominence.

## Visual World & Identity
- **Metaphor:** The Engineering Rack Workbench. Instead of a generic SaaS dashboard with cards, the interface feels like an interactive Network Operations Center (NOC) terminal where hardware, wiring, and telemetry are hands-on.
- **Surface & Material:** Deep matte obsidian grounds (`#050811`, `#090e1a`) with cold-rolled steel borders (`#151f32`, `#22314e`), tactical status indicators (Link-Up Green `#10b981`, Fiber Cyan `#06b6d4`, Copper Amber `#f59e0b`), and tactile copper pin contacts.
- **Typography:**
  - Display & Headings: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, tight tracking (-0.02em), heavy weight (700/800), clean geometric structure.
  - Body & Reading: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (400/500), high contrast (12:1 against background), calibrated 65ch measure.
  - Telemetry & Pinouts: `JetBrains Mono` for port designations, IEEE 802.3 standards, pin numbers, and timing metrics.

## Anti-Default Commitments
- ZERO generic purple-blue gradients on text.
- ZERO cards-inside-cards-inside-cards nesting.
- ZERO fake metric sparklines or meaningless decorative blur circles.
- ZERO emoji icons; all icons are precise, single-stroke engineered SVG vectors.
- NO eyebrow/kicker above headings; headings speak with distinct scale and typographic confidence.

## Component Language
1. **Workbench Topbar:** Tactical rack-mount header with live Link-Pulse, student callsign/level telemetry, and hardware mode switcher.
2. **Virtual 3D Stage:** Generous viewport framing the 3D `<model-viewer>` with interactive inspection controls (Orbit, Reset, Port Focus, Wireframe mode toggle).
3. **Tactical Micro-learning Reader:** Two-column split layout with interactive protocol diagrams, key takeaways, and inline Socratic challenges.
4. **8P8C Pinout Matrix:** Interactive wiring board displaying true TIA/EIA-568A and T568B pin assignments with physical wire cross-sections.
5. **Socratic AI Terminal:** Floating terminal with command-line prompt aesthetics, active lesson context indicator, and adaptive question generation.
